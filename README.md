   # Retail Performance Review (Excel)

   Status: 🚧 In progress (Week 1)

   ## Business question
   The COO asked: are we growing, are we making more money, are big trade customers going quiet, and are cancellations a problem?

   ## Data source
   UCI Online Retail dataset: https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/352/online+retail
   About 540K transactions from a UK online gift wholesaler, Dec 2010 – Dec 2011.
